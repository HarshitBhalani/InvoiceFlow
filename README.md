# InvoiceFlow

**Send invoices, get paid online, and always know who owes you what.**

InvoiceFlow is simple invoicing for freelancers, consultants and small teams. Create a professional invoice in a minute, email it to your client, let them pay by card, and watch your dashboard update on its own.

**Try it free:** click **Try the demo account** on the login page. No signup, no card. You get a private workspace full of sample data to explore.

---

## The problem

Most freelancers invoice from Word or Excel, send a PDF, and then wait. Did the client open it? Is it late? How much is still unpaid this month? Answering means digging through emails and spreadsheets, and chasing payments by hand.

## How InvoiceFlow fixes it

1. **Add your client once.** Name and email, and they are saved for next time.
2. **Create the invoice.** Add what you did, the price, tax and a due date. Totals are worked out for you.
3. **Send it.** Your client gets an email with a link to a clean invoice page.
4. **Get paid.** They pay by card right on that page. No account needed on their side.
5. **Relax.** The invoice turns **Paid** by itself. Late ones are marked **Overdue**, and reminders can go out for you automatically.

## What you can do

### Look professional
- Clean invoices with your **logo, brand color** and a choice of two templates
- Download as **PDF** or print in one click
- Tax support for **VAT, GST or sales tax**
- Bill in **USD, EUR, GBP, INR, CAD or AUD**

### Get paid faster
- **Email invoices** straight from the app. Client replies come to your inbox
- A **pay link** on every invoice so clients can pay by card instantly

- **Recurring invoices** for retainers and monthly clients. Set it once, it sends itself

### Know where your money is
- A **dashboard** showing what is paid, pending and overdue at a glance
- A **monthly revenue chart** and recent activity
- Track **expenses** and see a clear **profit and loss report** by month and category
- Export invoices and expenses to **CSV** for your accountant

### Easy to use
- Works on desktop and phone
- **Light and dark mode**
- A short guided tour on first login, and a built-in **help center** at `/docs`
